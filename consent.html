Messaging Consent, Opt-In, and Opt-Out Policy

TarHeelW Communications uses SMS and MMS messaging for service-related communication, appointment reminders, scheduling coordination, updates, confirmations, and direct customer follow-up. Our messaging program is intended for people who have directly requested communication from TarHeelW Communications.

How Consent Is Collected

Recipients provide their phone number directly to TarHeelW Communications and agree to receive messages through a direct request, written communication, appointment or service intake, or another documented opt-in process. We keep consent tied to the related customer interaction or request.

Standard Opt-In Workflow

  1. A customer initiates contact with TarHeelW Communications and requests reminders, scheduling help, updates, or follow-up communication.
  2. The customer provides a mobile phone number directly to TarHeelW Communications through a written request, intake form, email, or other direct communication.
  3. Before messaging begins, the customer is shown clear disclosure that TarHeelW Communications will send text messages for reminders, coordination, updates, or follow-up, that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, and that the customer can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
  4. The customer affirmatively agrees to receive those messages.
  5. TarHeelW Communications records the consent with the related request, intake, or customer communication before sending non-conversational follow-up messages.

Sample Opt-In Language

By providing your phone number and agreeing to receive text messages from TarHeelW Communications, you consent to receive reminders, coordination messages, updates, and service-related follow-up. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Sample Intake Checkbox Disclosure

[ ] I agree to receive text messages from TarHeelW Communications about reminders, scheduling coordination, updates, and service-related follow-up at the phone number I provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Sample Confirmation Message

TarHeelW Communications: You are opted in to receive reminders, scheduling messages, updates, and follow-up texts from TarHeelW Communications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Message Types

  • Appointment reminders
  • Scheduling coordination
  • Customer support follow-up
  • Service updates and confirmations

Opt-Out Policy

You may opt out of messaging at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, no further text messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

Customers may also request removal from messaging by emailing TarHeelW@icloud.com.

Help

You may reply HELP for help or contact TarHeelW@icloud.com.

Message Frequency and Fees

Message frequency varies depending on your requests, appointments, scheduling needs, and service activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent Recordkeeping

TarHeelW Communications keeps consent associated with the underlying customer interaction, intake, or written request so that messaging is sent only in connection with the service or follow-up the customer asked for.
