Customer Request and Contact Preferences

Customers may request communication by email, phone call, or SMS text. Agreeing to SMS is optional and is not required to submit a request.

This checkbox is optional and is shown unchecked by default. Customers who want email or phone call communication may submit a request without agreeing to SMS.

How consent is recorded

When a customer checks the SMS consent box and submits a request, TarHeelW Communications records that consent with the related customer interaction before non-conversational follow-up texting begins.
