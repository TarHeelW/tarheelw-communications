This page shows the full branded opt-in workflow used when a customer requests SMS reminders, scheduling coordination, confirmations, updates, or service-related follow-up from TarHeelW Communications.

Customer SMS Request Form

SMS consent is optional. Customers who do not want text messages may contact us by email instead at TarHeelW@icloud.com. This SMS request form requires an affirmative checkbox before SMS messages are sent.

Required SMS Consent Disclosure

The checkbox above is shown unchecked by default and must be selected by the customer before SMS opt-in is accepted. Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are linked below and in the footer.

SMS Program Disclosure

  • Business name: TarHeelW Communications
  • Messaging number: +1 (855) 592-4149
  • Message types: reminders, scheduling coordination, confirmations, updates, and service-related follow-up
  • Message frequency: varies by customer request and service activity
  • Fees: message and data rates may apply
  • Opt-out: reply STOP
  • Help: reply HELP or email TarHeelW@icloud.com

How consent is recorded

When a customer submits this SMS request, TarHeelW Communications keeps the customer request and SMS consent with the related interaction, including the customer’s name, phone number, request details, consent language, date/time, and source of opt-in.

Sample message

TarHeelW Communications: This is your requested scheduling update. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Privacy Policy | Terms of Service | Messaging Consent Policy

Opt-In Workflow Summary for Compliance Review

  1. The customer visits this TarHeelW Communications branded SMS opt-in page.
  2. The customer enters their name, mobile number, email address, and request details.
  3. The customer reviews the full SMS disclosure, including message types, frequency, fees, STOP, HELP, privacy, and terms links.
  4. The customer affirmatively checks the unchecked SMS consent box.
  5. The customer submits the SMS opt-in request, and TarHeelW Communications keeps the consent record with the related customer interaction.
