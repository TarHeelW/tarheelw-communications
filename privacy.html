Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 26, 2026

TarHeelW Communications respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit this website, contact us, or receive messages from us. TarHeelW Communications is a Texas-based customer communication and reminder service that sends service-related communications only to people who directly request or agree to receive them.

Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, email address, and communication details you provide to us directly.
  • Service, scheduling, reminder, support, and follow-up records reasonably related to your request.
  • Consent and opt-in records associated with messaging requests and customer interactions.
  • Basic website or communication details needed to respond to requests and maintain reliable service.

How We Use Information

  • To send requested reminders, scheduling messages, service updates, confirmations, and customer follow-up.
  • To respond to support requests and communication questions.
  • To maintain security, reliability, operational records, and compliance for our messaging program.

SMS Privacy Statement

SMS opt-in data, phone numbers, and consent records are not sold or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers and platform partners as reasonably needed to deliver messaging services, process requests, comply with legal obligations, or protect rights and safety.

Consent Records

TarHeelW Communications keeps consent associated with the customer request, intake, written communication, or other direct interaction that led to the messaging relationship.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, support, compliance, recordkeeping, and operational purposes.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures are used to protect the information we maintain. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may also contact us to ask questions about your communication preferences or the information associated with your request.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or TarHeelW Communications messaging practices, contact TarHeelW@icloud.com.
