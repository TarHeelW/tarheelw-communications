About TarHeelW Communications

TarHeelW Communications provides person-to-person business communication support for customers who want clear reminders, confirmation messages, and follow-up updates. Our messaging program is used for practical service communication, not for unsolicited marketing.

Customers hear from TarHeelW Communications only after they directly provide their phone number and request or agree to receive text communication related to reminders, scheduling, updates, or follow-up.

How Our SMS Messaging Works

TarHeelW Communications sends SMS or MMS messages only to people who have provided their phone number directly and agreed to receive communication for reminders, scheduling coordination, service updates, confirmations, or direct follow-up.

  • The customer initiates the interaction and provides a mobile number directly to TarHeelW Communications.
  • Before messaging begins, the customer is told what kinds of texts will be sent.
  • Disclosures include that message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and that the customer can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
  • Consent is recorded with the related request, intake, or written communication before follow-up texting begins.

Consent and Compliance

We maintain messaging consent records through direct customer interactions, written requests, appointment or service intake, and other documented opt-in processes. We do not send marketing text messages to people who have not provided consent.

For a full description of our opt-in, opt-out, help, and consent workflow, please see our Messaging Consent page.
