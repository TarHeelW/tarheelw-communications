Who We Serve

How Customers Reach Us

Customers contact TarHeelW Communications directly by email or through a service interaction that includes a request for communication. When a customer asks for reminders, scheduling help, or follow-up, they may provide a mobile number for service-related texting.

Questions about messaging, privacy, or support can be sent to TarHeelW@icloud.com.

How Our SMS Messaging Works

TarHeelW Communications sends SMS or MMS messages only to people who directly provide their phone number and agree to receive communication related to reminders, scheduling coordination, updates, confirmations, or follow-up.

  • The customer initiates contact or requests follow-up.
  • The customer provides a mobile number directly to TarHeelW Communications.
  • The customer is told the kinds of messages they may receive.
  • The customer is informed that message frequency varies and message/data rates may apply.
  • The customer can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
  • Consent is documented with the related request, intake, or written communication.

Examples of Messages We Send

  • "TarHeelW Communications: This is your reminder for tomorrow's scheduled appointment. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help."
  • "TarHeelW Communications: We are following up on your request and confirming your scheduled time. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help."
  • "TarHeelW Communications: Your requested update is available. We will follow up if you need additional help. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help."
