How Our SMS Messaging Works

TarHeelW Communications sends SMS or MMS messages only to people who have provided their phone number directly and agreed to receive communication for reminders, coordination, service updates, or direct follow-up.

  • Message frequency varies based on the customer relationship and service activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help.

Consent and Compliance

We maintain messaging consent records through direct customer interactions, written requests, or other documented opt-in processes. We do not send marketing text messages to people who have not provided consent.
